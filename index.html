The Society

An Institution Built on Principle & Purpose

GCES is a Government Registered computer education society founded on a simple conviction — that computer literacy is a right, not a privilege. We deliver education with the rigour of an academy and the warmth of a community.

  • 01
    Government Registered

    A recognised society whose certificates carry weight with employers and institutions.

  • 02
    Complete Curriculum

    From Computer Fundamentals to Programming, Accounting, DTP and Multimedia — all under one roof.

  • 03
    For Every Learner

    School, college, professional and open-university students — plus ITI and industrial training.

  • 04
    Beyond the Classroom

    Project development, software development, website development, typing and printing services.
